AI video production for brands: what it is and how it works

AI video production explained for brands: how AI-generated commercials and films are made, what a director still does, and what to prepare.

“AI video” often sounds like pressing a button and getting a commercial. Real AI video production for brands is closer to filmmaking than to magic: a director, a script and many decisions — with AI generating the footage instead of a camera.

What AI does — and what it does not

AI models can generate shots from descriptions and reference images: landscapes, interiors, products, camera moves, light. They do not decide what the story is, which shot is right, how scenes connect or what the brand should feel like. That is direction, and it is still human work.

The process

  • Idea and script — the same as in any film or commercial.
  • Look development — style frames, characters and locations are designed and kept consistent across shots.
  • Generation — each shot is generated, reviewed and regenerated until it serves the scene.
  • Post-production — edit, voice, music, sound design and colour grading turn shots into a film.

Where AI production is strongest

Worlds that are expensive or impossible to film: space, deep history, remote nature, fantasy. Perfect product shots without a studio day. Many versions of the same commercial for different markets and formats.

Where to be careful

Real people, real locations and exact brand assets need care: references, approvals and, where needed, real footage combined with generated shots. A responsible studio is open about what is generated and respects rights and likeness.

What a brand should prepare

A short brief, the audience, where the video will run, brand guidelines (logo, colours, fonts) and any product photos or 3D files. One sentence of the idea is enough to start; the rest is shaped together.
