Fashion and beauty campaigns: films, lookbooks and social content with AI

How fashion and beauty brands create campaign films, lookbooks and social content — mood, colour, texture and formats — with classic shoots and AI.

Fashion and beauty live on images. A season, a collection or a new fragrance needs a campaign film, stills for a lookbook and a stream of content for social media — all in one recognisable mood.

Start with the mood, not the product

Strong fashion campaigns sell a world first: a place, a light, a feeling. The collection or the product then belongs to that world. A mood board and style frames fix this world before production begins.

Texture and colour

Fabric, skin, liquid, glass — texture is everything in fashion and beauty. Macro shots, slow motion and careful colour grading make materials feel tangible. A consistent palette ties the film, the lookbook and social posts together.

The formats a campaign needs

  • A campaign film — 30 to 90 seconds.
  • Short vertical cuts for Reels and TikTok.
  • Stills for the lookbook, website and marketplaces.
  • Loops for product pages and screens in stores.

Where AI helps

AI production is useful for worlds and atmospheres that are hard to shoot — surreal sets, impossible locations, abstract visuals of colour and liquid — and for producing many versions quickly. Campaigns built around real people and real garments usually combine a classic shoot with generated environments, with clear consent and rights for everyone shown.

Consistency is the luxury

What makes a campaign feel premium is not one spectacular shot but consistency: the same light, rhythm and restraint across every format.
