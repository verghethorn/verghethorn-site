Brand collaborations and product placement: when the ad is the story

How brand collaborations and product placement work in films and online content — natural integration, story first, approvals and distribution.

Viewers skip ads but watch stories. Brand collaborations put a brand inside content people actually want to see — a short film, a series or a creator’s video — instead of interrupting it.

Product placement vs. collaboration

Classic product placement puts a product on screen. A collaboration goes further: the brand’s values, product or place become part of the plot, and the content is created together with the brand from the start.

Story first

The most effective branded content works even without the brand — and becomes better with it. If the story only exists to show the product, viewers feel it. A natural role for the brand is the goal.

How it works

  • The brand shares its values, audience and boundaries.
  • The studio proposes a story with a natural role for the brand.
  • The brand approves the story and the look before production.
  • The premiere is planned together on both sides’ channels.

Why it pays off

People share stories that move them. A brand inside such a story travels with that feeling — further than a banner and with more trust than a pre-roll ad.
