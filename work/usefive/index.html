UseFive

An app you talk to. Personal AI agents with distinct characters and their own voices.

Status
OriginalIn development
Year
2026
Role
Author: product concept, character and voice direction, interface, teaser film
Format
Mobile app · 9:16 teaser film

Idea

Agents with character, not chatbots.

Two agents to start: one designed for brothers, one for sisters. Each has a face, a voice and a manner of speaking of its own.

Visual solution

A hood, a visor, one line of light.

Faceless hooded figures with an LED visor — blue dots for the brother, pink for the sister — lit by a warm amber rim. Recognisable at icon size, cinematic at full screen.

Teaser film

0:00 / 0:30

Vertical teaser, 30 seconds, no sound track.

Try the interface

Choose an agent and hear its voice. The prototype is an interface study for the app — the states are real: ready, connecting, speaking, finished.

The brother’s voice is generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech and directed by VERGHÉTHORN. The sister’s voice is in production.
