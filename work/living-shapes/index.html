Living Shapes

Five heroes made of real materials. Each episode asks “What if…?” — and answers with a real experiment.

Status
OriginalIn development
Year
2026
Role
Author: concept, story bible, character direction, scripts, AI-assisted production
Format
16:9 for YouTube · 9:16 for Shorts, Reels, TikTok · English

Task

Make physics feel physical.

Short-format science that an audience of 18–34 watches for the story and remembers for the science. No lecture voice, no stock animation.

Idea

Each hero owns a branch of physics.

Every episode opens with a “What if…?” hook, moves from a CGI adventure to a real experiment filmed on camera, and closes with an explanation through the lens of Verghe.

Visual solution

Macro photography, not cartoons.

The heroes look like real objects on a lab bench: real materials, shallow depth of field, photographic light. They have no mouths — emotion lives in the eyes, posture and floating hands, joined to the body by a glowing ring in each hero’s colour.

Sound is material only: chimes, clicks, bubbles. No speech.

Meet the five

Choose a hero to bring it into focus.

Five heroes, five materials, five colours.

Where it is now

In development. The story bible and the five heroes are designed; test shorts are in production for YouTube, Shorts, Reels and TikTok.
