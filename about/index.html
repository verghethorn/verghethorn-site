About

A practice, not an agency.

VERGHÉTHORN

VERGHÉTHORN is the creative practice of Rashid Kurabegov. It began with a simple conviction: a story should not be locked into one medium. The same idea can be a film, a campaign, a website or an app — and each form should feel made by the same hand.

Current work includes Living Shapes, an original series of physics stories told by heroes made of real materials, and UseFive, an app built around AI voice agents with distinct characters.

AI is part of the toolkit — for look development, shots, voices and code — in the way a camera or an edit suite is. Authorship, taste and responsibility for the result stay human.

Principles

  1. 01No invented proof

    No fake clients, awards, reviews or numbers. Every project is shown with its real status.

  2. 02Story before effect

    An effect earns its place by carrying meaning, not by being new.

  3. 03Craft that lasts

    A visual language that will still feel right in five years, not a collage of this season’s trends.
