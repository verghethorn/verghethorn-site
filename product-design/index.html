Questions? Answers.

What kinds of products do you design?

Objects people use and love — tableware, shoes, lamps, accessories and more.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually. Start a project and you will receive a personal proposal.

Do you work with manufacturers?

Yes. Files are prepared together with your manufacturer for production.

Can you also make the launch film?

Yes — product design and the launch film can be one project.

Product design, 3D visualisation and product videos.

We design physical products — tableware, shoes, lamps, accessories — from the first sketch to production files, with 3D models and photoreal renders you can use for presentations and pre-orders.

When the product is ready, the same team can produce the product video or launch commercial, so the design and the way it is shown to the world belong together.
