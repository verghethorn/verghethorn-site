How we work

Simple for you. Precise inside.

You talk about the goal. We take care of the technology — and explain every decision in plain words.

The process

  1. 01

    Listen

    A conversation about your goal, your audience and your deadline. In your words, without jargon.

  2. 02

    Show

    You see the idea before production begins: a script, a design or a working prototype.

  3. 03

    Create

    Film, design, code and voice — with checkpoints where you can steer.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Launch, final files and a clear handover, so the result keeps working for you.

The technology, in plain words

  1. 01Artificial intelligence

    The newest models for images, video, voice and code. They give speed and freedom; a human director makes every decision.

  2. 02Real-time graphics

    Light, 3D and motion running live in the browser — the same family of technology as modern games, made light enough for a phone.

  3. 03Built for every device

    Everything is tested on phones and computers, works in several languages, and stays readable for everyone.

Questions

What does a project cost?

Every project is different. After a short conversation you receive a clear proposal — scope, timing and price — before any work begins.

How long does it take?

It depends on the form: a short film, a website and an app take different time. The timeline is fixed in the proposal.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. You describe the goal in your own words. We handle the technology and explain each step simply.

Which languages do you work in?

Russian and English.

Is content made with AI safe to use?

We use AI tools whose terms allow commercial use, and we tell you openly which parts were made with them.
