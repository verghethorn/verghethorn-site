01 — Method

Animated on the outside.
Accurate on the inside.

The characters are cartoons. What happens to them is not. Every episode takes one real phenomenon, shows it on camera or in measured data, and explains it in the voice of the material it's happening to.

One real phenomenon

Piezoelectricity, shear thickening, elasticity, fluorescence. Each episode is built around a single effect you can reproduce yourself.

Shown, then explained

Real footage and real numbers first. Animation is used for what a camera can't see: atoms, bonds, fields and forces.

Sourced and checked

Scripts are checked against textbooks and peer-reviewed literature. Sources go in the description of every video.

02 — The cast

ZevvoCitrine quartz

Real citrine quartz

Silicon dioxide, SiO₂. Its yellow colour comes from traces of iron. Quartz is piezoelectric: squeeze it and it produces a voltage, apply a voltage and it vibrates. That's why Zevvo runs on electricity.

32,768 Hzhow fast the quartz crystal in a watch vibrates to keep time
02 / 05
LumoHydrogel
Specimen slotpolymer + H₂OHover Lumo to split
Real hydrogel

A network of cross-linked polymer chains that traps water. It holds its shape while being mostly liquid. Hydrogels are used in soft contact lenses, wound dressings and the absorbent core of nappies.

>90%water by mass in many hydrogels, and they still hold their shape
03 / 05
KrodoPBT plastic keycap
Specimen slot(C₁₂H₁₂O₄)ₙHover Krodo to split
Real keyboard keycaps

Polybutylene terephthalate (PBT), a thermoplastic polyester. It's tough, resists heat and wears without going shiny under fingers. That's why premium keycaps are made from it.

≈223 °Cmelting point of PBT, so it can take hits and heat
04 / 05
ThorneSpring steel
Specimen slotFe + CHover Thorne to split
Real steel springs

Iron alloyed with about 0.5–1% carbon. Inside the elastic limit its atomic bonds stretch and spring back, and the force grows in proportion to the stretch. That's Hooke's law.

F = −kxHooke's law: the restoring force is proportional to the stretch
05 / 05
VergheFluorite
Specimen slotCaF₂Hover Verghe to split
Real fluorite

Calcium fluoride, CaF₂. It splits cleanly into octahedra and bends light with very low dispersion, which is why it's used in high-end camera and telescope lenses. Many samples glow under UV light.

1852the year G. G. Stokes named fluorescence after fluorite
03 — Experiment 01 · in production

The liquid that fights back.

Mix cornstarch with water and you get a non-Newtonian fluid. Push into it slowly and you sink. Hit it fast and the starch grains jam together, so for an instant it acts like a solid. This is shear thickening.
