Questions? Answers.

How is a collaboration different from a commercial?

A commercial is about your product. A collaboration is a story people watch for itself — with your brand naturally inside it.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually. Start a project and you will receive a personal proposal.

Do we approve the story?

Yes. You see the story and the style frames before production starts.

Where will the film be published?

On VERGHÉTHORN channels and on yours — we plan the premiere together.

Branded content and product placement, done naturally.

A brand collaboration is branded content that people watch for the story: your product, place or values become part of an original film instead of an ad break. It is product placement that feels natural.

We propose the story, you approve it, and the film premieres on VERGHÉTHORN channels and yours — with versions for YouTube, Reels, TikTok and your website.
