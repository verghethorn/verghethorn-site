Questions? Answers.

How long does a film take?

It depends on the length and the story. After a short conversation the director proposes a timeline for your project.

How much does it cost?

Every film is quoted individually. Start a project and you will receive a personal proposal.

What do you need from me?

An idea, a feeling or a brief. Even one sentence is enough to begin — the rest we shape together.

Is everything made by AI?

AI generates the footage. The story, direction, edit, sound and colour are made by a filmmaker.

Film production for brands and storytellers.

We produce short films, brand films and series: from the first idea and script to characters, locations, sound, music and colour grading. AI generates the footage; a director shapes every scene, so the result feels like cinema, not like a template.

A brand film tells who you are without selling directly. A short film can launch a channel, a campaign or a festival run. Both are delivered in every format — 16:9 for cinema and YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 1:1 for feeds.
