Lab

Things you can touch.

Three working demonstrations. Each starts only when you ask it to, works with touch and keyboard, and never blocks the rest of the site.

A / Lab

Reality Shift

The Living Frame engine with its controls exposed. Scrub from film to structure to interface, and open or close the aperture.

Loads a small WebGL scene (about 70 KB image).

B / Lab

Motion Language

Seven coupled springs. Pull one and watch the others answer. Stiffness and damping are the two numbers behind every animation on this site.

Keyboard: focus a spring, press ↑ or ↓; ← → move between springs.

C / Lab

Playable Product

A tiny storyboard app. Add shots, rename them, set durations and reorder — runtime and timeline update live and are saved in this browser.

    Reorder with the number handle: drag, or focus it and press ↑ ↓.
