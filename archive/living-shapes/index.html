Idea

Each hero owns a branch of physics.

Every episode opens with a “What if…?” hook, moves from a CGI adventure to a real experiment on camera, and closes with an explanation through the lens of Verghe.

Look

Macro photography, not cartoons.

The heroes look like real objects on a lab bench: real materials, shallow depth of field, photographic light. No mouths — emotion lives in the eyes, posture and floating hands.
