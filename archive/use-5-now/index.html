Idea

Constant good, so small you never switch it off.

A person chooses their country and an official recipient — a mosque or a registered fund — tops up a wallet once, and walks. Each step is worth a fraction of a cent. Once a day the sum goes to the recipient in a single transfer.

Principles

Trust built into the interface.

The app never holds money — a licensed partner does. 0% commission, stated on the wallet screen. Giving stays private: rankings compare steps, never amounts. No reminders to top up, no pressure.

In four steps

  1. 01

    Top up

    Once, with a local card or wallet.

  2. 02

    Walk

    Every step adds a fraction of a cent.

  3. 03

    Daily total

    At one time for the whole country, the day’s sum is sent.

  4. 04

    Confirmation

    The recipient confirms and says what it will fund.
