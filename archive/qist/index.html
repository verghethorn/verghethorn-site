Honest installments. Everything visible, nothing forgotten.

Year

Role

Format
Mobile app · web prototype

Task

Replace a notebook with clarity.

A business that sells goods in installments under murabaha rules needs to see, at any moment, who has paid, who is late and what is coming — and its clients need to see the same truth from their side.

Solution

Two views of one truth.

The owner sees the money to receive, payments waiting for confirmation and overdue deals. The client sees the next payment, what is left and each purchase. Large numbers, plain words, one action per screen.

Try the prototype

Sign in as the owner or as a client. All names and amounts are invented examples.
